Health Assist reads Apple Health on your iPhone and sends the readings to a webhook in your Home Assistant.

The app and the integration are separate, free, MIT-licensed pieces. Install the integration in Home Assistant, create a webhook, then paste the URL (or scan its QR code) into the app. The phone must be able to reach Home Assistant directly: the same Wi-Fi, a VPN such as Tailscale, or a remote address such as Nabu Casa. If the phone cannot reach that address, the sync fails.

With read-only Apple Health access, the app sends steps, sleep, heart rate, weight and other readings. They appear as Home Assistant sensors with units and long-term statistics. The first sync sends all available history; later syncs send new data, and you can re-export everything from the app.

There is no account, no analytics, no telemetry and no vendor cloud. The app never writes to Apple Health. The webhook URL and optional shared secret are stored in the iOS Keychain.

WHAT YOU NEED
• Home Assistant 2024.1 or newer
• iOS 18 or newer
• The Health Assist integration, installed with HACS or manually
• A webhook URL and optional shared secret
