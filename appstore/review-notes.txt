Health Assist is a companion app for Home Assistant, the open-source home
automation platform that the user runs themselves. The app has no account and
no developer-operated backend. The developer receives no data.

WHAT THE APP DOES
The app reads Apple Health data with HealthKit, read-only, and POSTs it to a
webhook URL the user configures on their own Home Assistant instance. Home
Assistant creates sensors and long-term statistics from that data.

HOW TO TEST WITHOUT A HOME ASSISTANT SERVER
The app includes a "Send a Test Payload" action that sends fixed sample values
and does not read HealthKit. Use the demo webhook below.

  Demo webhook URL: <DEMO_WEBHOOK_URL>
  Shared secret:    <DEMO_SECRET or leave blank>

The demo endpoint accepts any POST, stores nothing and returns
{"accepted":1,"ignored":0}.

1. Open the app and paste the demo webhook URL into "Home Assistant".
2. Leave the shared secret blank unless a value is given above.
3. Tap "Send a Test Payload" and confirm the Result section reports success.
4. On a device, tap "Request Health Access" to see the HealthKit permission
   sheet. Read-only access is requested; the app never writes to Apple Health.
5. Tap "Sync Now" to exercise the full HealthKit path.

HEALTHKIT
HealthKit is used read-only to read the metric types the app displays. Health
data is not used for advertising, is not shared with third parties other than
the user's own Home Assistant endpoint, and is not collected by the developer.

BACKGROUND MODES
UIBackgroundModes "fetch", the BGTaskScheduler weekly fallback and the HealthKit
background-delivery entitlement keep data current while the app is closed. These
are core functionality, not background location or audio.

LOCAL NETWORK
The app connects to the user's Home Assistant over the local network.
NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription explains the system prompt.
