# Support Home Assistant Health Bridge

Health Bridge is free and open source. There is no paid tier, no account and no
subscription.

## Support My Work

The iOS app has an optional **Tip Jar** using Apple's in-app purchase. Tips are
optional and unlock nothing. You can also support ongoing maintenance through
[GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/thebillington) from this page.
Support is optional and does not unlock any features.

## Report a problem

Open an issue at
[thebillington/health-assist](https://github.com/thebillington/health-assist/issues).
On TestFlight you can also send feedback straight from the TestFlight app.

Include:

- the Home Assistant version;
- the Health Bridge integration version;
- whether the problem happens on a manual sync, a background sync, or both;
- the relevant Home Assistant log lines with secrets removed.

Never paste your webhook URL or shared secret into an issue. Treat both as
credentials.

## Before you report

- Check [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
- Confirm the webhook URL is the full URL shown in the integration, not just the
  path.
- Try a manual **Sync Now** from the iOS app and check the app's result message.
- Use **Send a Test Payload** in the app to separate an app problem from a Home
  Assistant problem.
